---
NAME: local_macro_scope_independence
DESCRIPTION: Local label .foo under global is independent from .foo in macro
INPUT:
 1: * = $0200
 2: global:
 3: .foo
 4:   LDA #$01
 5:   .macro TESTMACRO
 6:   LDA #$02
 7: .foo
 8:   LDA #$03
 9:   .endmacro
10:   JMP .foo     ; Should jump to global's .foo at $0200
11:   TESTMACRO
EXPECT_HEX: a9 01 4c 00 02 a9 02 a9 03
